# Code of Conduct

This applies to every repository under [github.com/odal-node](https://github.com/odal-node)
and to every space the project runs — issues, pull requests, discussions, code review,
commit messages, and email to the project's addresses.

## The standard

Be straightforward and be decent. Concretely:

- **Criticise the work, not the person.** "This is wrong because X" is the whole job.
  "You are careless" is not a review.
- **Say what you actually think, and say why.** Blunt technical disagreement is welcome
  and expected. Vagueness to avoid friction wastes everyone's time.
- **Accept being wrong in public.** Everyone here is wrong regularly; the record of this
  project is partly a record of corrections. Correcting yourself is not a loss of face.
- **Assume the other person read the same thing differently**, not that they are acting
  in bad faith — until they demonstrate otherwise.

Unacceptable, in any volume or tone:

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments about anyone.
- Discrimination or slurs on any basis — including who someone is, where they are from,
  and how well they write English.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention of any kind.
- Sustained disruption: reopening settled decisions to wear people down, bad-faith
  process argument, deliberate derailing.

## A note specific to this project

This is compliance infrastructure. **Knowingly asserting something false about EU law,
about what the software does, or about its production readiness is a conduct problem
here, not merely a technical error.** Operators may act on what this project says. Get
it wrong honestly as often as you like; do not get it wrong on purpose, and do not
present a plan in the grammar of a shipped feature.

## Reporting

Report to **contact@odal-node.io**. Reports are read by the project maintainer.

Include what happened, where, and when — a link is enough. You do not need to have a
remedy in mind.

**What you can expect:** acknowledgement within 72 hours, and a decision communicated to
you. Your report will not be shared with the person you are reporting without telling
you first. If your report concerns the maintainer, you have no internal escalation path
here — say so in your report, and GitHub's own
[abuse reporting](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
is available to you independently of this project.

**Being honest about scale:** this project currently has one maintainer. There is no
committee, no rotation, and no appeals body. That is a real limitation and it is stated
here rather than implied by a document that reads as though a team exists.

## Consequences

Depending on severity: a private correction, a public correction, removal of the
offending content, a temporary block, or a permanent ban from the organization's
repositories. Maintainers who do not uphold this document in good faith are subject to
it themselves.
