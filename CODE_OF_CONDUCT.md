# Code of Conduct

This applies to every repository under [github.com/odal-node](https://github.com/odal-node)
and to every space the project runs: issues, pull requests, discussions, code review,
commit messages, and email to the project's addresses.

## The standard

Be direct and be decent:

- **Criticise the work, not the person.** "This is wrong because X" is a review.
  "You are careless" is not.
- **Say what you think, and why.** Blunt technical disagreement is welcome. Vagueness
  to avoid friction wastes everyone's time.
- **Accept being wrong in public.** Everyone here is wrong regularly, and much of this
  project's history is corrections. Correcting yourself costs nothing.
- **Assume the other person read the same thing differently,** not that they are
  acting in bad faith, until they show otherwise.

Not acceptable, in any volume or tone:

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments about anyone.
- Discrimination or slurs on any basis, including who someone is, where they are from,
  and how well they write English.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention of any kind.
- Sustained disruption: reopening settled decisions to wear people down, bad-faith
  process arguments, deliberate derailing.

## Accuracy about the law and the software

This software is used for regulatory compliance, and operators may act on what the
project says. **Knowingly stating something false about EU law, about what the
software does, or about its production readiness is a conduct problem here,** not only
a technical error. Mistakes are fine. Misstatements on purpose are not, and neither is
describing a plan as a shipped feature.

## Reporting

Report to **contact@odal-node.io**. The project maintainer reads every report.

Say what happened, where and when. A link is enough, and you do not need to propose a
remedy.

You get an acknowledgement within 72 hours and a decision afterwards. Your report is
not shared with the person you report without telling you first.

The project has one maintainer, so there is no committee, rotation or appeals body. If
your report concerns the maintainer, say so in the report. GitHub's
[abuse reporting](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
is available to you independently of this project.

## Consequences

Depending on severity: a private correction, a public correction, removal of the
content, a temporary block, or a permanent ban from the organization's repositories.
The maintainer is bound by this document too.
