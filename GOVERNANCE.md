# Governance

The organization-wide default for Odal Node. `dpp-core` and `dpp-engine` publish their
own `GOVERNANCE.md`, which applies in those repositories.

## Who decides

**Aleksandar Temelkov** is the sole maintainer and the sole organization owner. One
person decides technical direction, what merges, what is released and what the
licences are. There is no steering committee, technical board, vote or appeals
process.

## What that means for contributors

- **A proposal can be declined without a formal process.** You get a reason. You do
  not get a vote.
- **The bus factor is one.** If this account is lost, the organization, its
  repositories and its billing go with it. This is a known, open risk.
- **The project is not a foundation and is not vendor-neutral.** `dpp-core` is
  Apache-2.0 so that using it does not depend on this governance at all.

## What the licences guarantee

The regulatory library, `dpp-core`, is Apache-2.0 and stays that way. If the project
stops, anyone can keep using, forking and vendoring it without further permission.

`dpp-engine` is BSL-1.1: source-available, free to self-host for your own compliance
obligations, and converting to Apache-2.0 on the change date stated in its `LICENSE`.
The `LICENSE` file is the authoritative text.

## How this document changes

If the project gains maintainers, this document is rewritten before they get merge
rights.
